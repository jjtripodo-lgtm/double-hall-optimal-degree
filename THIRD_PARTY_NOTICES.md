# Dependencies and source rights

The source package pins its Lean and Lake dependencies in DEPENDENCIES.json
and companion/lake-manifest.json. Pinned upstream license texts are supplied
under licenses/. Lean and the external packages are obtained separately;
compiled objects and upstream library sources are not bundled here.

The Cli dependency uses the MIT License; the other listed Lake packages use
Apache-2.0. Their licenses do not license the original research sources.
The manuscript's citations identify mathematical antecedents separately.
Original manuscript/code license grants are defined in LICENSE.
